Final Playbook

The next career strategy is not one thing.

Job Seeker

Build a portfolio around Python, TypeScript, and SQL that makes your AI-assisted workflow visible and reviewable.

Junior Developer

Protect debugging practice, tests, and code-reading depth. Do not let generation replace understanding.

Senior Developer

Lean into architecture, risk, data quality, and mentoring: the skills that judge AI output.
