Career Path

Skills have to land somewhere.

After the skill redraft, move to roles. Salary and experience show where the preparation pays off: some tracks are direct AI roles, while others are judgment-heavy roles made more valuable by AI adoption.

Job Seeker

Build a portfolio around Python, TypeScript, SQL/data, and one deployable AI-assisted product. Show review notes, not just generated code.

Junior

Use AI daily, but make debugging, testing, and reading unfamiliar code the core skill. The weakness to beat is "almost right" output.

Senior

Move toward architecture, security, data quality, and mentoring. AI makes execution cheaper, so judgment and accountability become the premium layer.
