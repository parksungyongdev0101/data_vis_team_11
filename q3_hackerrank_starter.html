Where do developers go in the AI era?

Hiring appears to be recovering, but the entry point for early-career developers is narrowing. AI is accelerating development while raising expectations for faster output. This report redrafts the skills and roles developers should prepare for next.

Executive Summary

Hiring is recovering, but the entry point still feels narrow for early-career developers.
Developers are not waiting for companies to invest in them. Without growth, they move.
AI accelerates work, but the gap between deep adopters and casual users is widening.
A strong career strategy is not one tool. It combines skills, verification habits, and role choices.
Skills are being redrafted. AI adjacency, growth, and practical context matter beyond popularity.

Final Playbook

The next career strategy is not one thing.

Job Seeker

Build a portfolio around Python, TypeScript, and SQL that makes your AI-assisted workflow visible.

Junior Developer

Do not only show AI usage. Prove debugging, verification, and code review habits.

Senior Developer

Lean into architecture, security, data quality, and mentoring: the skills that judge AI output.
